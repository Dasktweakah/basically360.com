Windows dashboard · hobby project

your PC, basically a 360.

Basically360 is a full-screen, controller-first dashboard for Windows PCs, styled after the Xbox 360 dashboard. It brings every game you own into one place, and you can use all of it from the couch.

  • Windows 10 & 11 · 64-bit
  • Xbox controller
  • Runs on your PC
  • Free · not for sale

What it does

everything on one dashboard

Basically360 replaces the Windows desktop while you're playing. It looks and sounds like the 360's 2011 dashboard, and it launches games from all the places a modern PC keeps them.

Library

my games

Games from the Xbox app, Steam and EA in one list with box art, along with Xbox 360 games run through the Xenia emulator and GameCube games through Dolphin. Add-ons and downloadable content appear under their game.

Profile

xbox live sign-in

Sign in with a Microsoft account to show your gamertag, gamerpic, Gamerscore and achievements.

Social

friends & messages

See which friends are online and read your messages, through Xbox and Discord.

Store

games marketplace

Browse top games, deals, new releases, demos and PC Game Pass, then buy in the Microsoft Store.

Feed

activity alerts

A feed of recent friend activity: achievements earned, games played and new messages.

Search

one search

Search your own games and apps, the Marketplace, YouTube and the web from one on-screen keyboard.

Guide

guide button

Press the guide button during any game to reach the dashboard, your friends or the power options.

Apps

controller as mouse

In Windows and in your apps, the controller works as a mouse. In games it goes back to being a controller.

Retro

gamecube menu

Boots the GameCube's own start-up menu, and the bumpers change discs.

Movies & TV

browse films & shows

Browse trending, popular and new films and shows, or everything on one streaming service. See trailers and every service a title is on, and open it in that service.

How it works

from power button to playing

Basically360 is one small Windows program with nothing to install around it. It doesn't run anything in the background once you close it.

  1. Start it

    Basically360 opens full screen, plays its start-up sequence and lands on home.

  2. It finds your games

    It reads which games are installed for the Xbox app, Steam, EA, Xenia and Dolphin, then shows them with their covers.

  3. Sign in if you like

    Signing in to Xbox LIVE or Discord is optional. Each one opens that service's own sign-in page in your browser.

  4. Pick a game

    The game launches and Basically360 steps aside. The guide button brings it back.

Privacy

your data stays on your PC

What Basically360 collects

Nothing. It has no analytics, no ads, no tracking and no accounts of its own. It has no servers, so nothing you do in it is sent to the developer.

What is stored, and where

  • Your settings, in a plain text file next to the program.
  • Sign-in tokens for Xbox LIVE and Discord, encrypted with Windows' own data protection so only your Windows account can read them.
  • Cached cover art and store details, so pages open quickly.

Signing in

Basically360 never sees your password. Sign-in happens on the service's own page in your browser, and the service gives back a token that only allows what's listed under services. Signing out deletes the stored token.

Removing it

Delete the Basically360 folder and everything it stored is gone. You can also remove its access from your Microsoft or Discord account settings at any time.
